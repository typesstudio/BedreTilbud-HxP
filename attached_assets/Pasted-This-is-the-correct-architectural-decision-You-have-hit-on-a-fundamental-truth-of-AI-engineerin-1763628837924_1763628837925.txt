We are moving to a **Enrichment Pattern**. The code handles the facts; the AI handles the feelings (narrative).

### The Replit Prompt

**Subject:** Refactor Comparison Service to Protect Deterministic Data

**Context:**
We are refactoring the `ComparisonService`. Currently, we send calculated coverage rows to the AI and ask it to return them in the final JSON. The AI consistently simplifies or deletes these rows. We need to change this to a "Merge Pattern."

**Technical Strategy:**

1.  **Deterministic Layer:** The code calculates `coverage_rows`, `highlights`, and `numeric_savings`. We keep these in a variable (`deterministicData`).
2.  **AI Layer:** We send the policy data to the AI but ask *only* for narrative fields (`analysis_text`, `good_to_know`, `missing_information_warnings`).
3.  **Merge Layer:** The code combines `deterministicData` + `aiResponse` into the final object to save to the database.

**Task:**
Update `server/services/comparisonService.ts` and `server/agents/comparisonAgent.ts`.

**Step 1: Update `comparisonAgent.ts` (The Prompt)**

      * `recommendations` (array of strings)
      * `missing_information` (array of strings)
      * `good_to_know` (string - optional)
  * The AI should focus purely on the qualitative comparison (strengths, weaknesses, gaps).

### Why this works

1.  **Zero Latency on Rows:** Your table generation becomes instantaneous because it's just code.
2.  **100% Integrity:** The AI literally cannot delete a row because it never touches the array.
3.  **Focus:** The AI prompt becomes much shorter and clearer ("Analyze these two policies and tell me which is better"), leading to better narratives.
